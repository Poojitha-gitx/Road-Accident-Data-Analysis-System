Academic Governance, Privacy & Methodological Disclosures

This system was engineered as a university academic project in Computer Science & Engineering. It is dedicated to methodological transparency, ethical computational practices, and data integrity:

Synthetic Demonstration Dataset: Recorded incidents represent synthesized demonstration samples spanning urban regional sectors in Andhra Pradesh (Tirupati, Chittoor, Madanapalle, Pileru, Srikalahasti, Puttur, Palamaner). All road names depict simulated sector corridors created for reproducible academic demonstration.

Privacy & Non-Causality: The system maintains strict zero personally identifying information (PII)—no personal identities, vehicle license plates, or driver information are stored. All analytical observations demonstrate statistical and empirical associations, not direct causal factors.
